What is an ignition interlock device?

An ignition interlock device (IID) is a small breath-alcohol tester wired directly into your vehicle's ignition. Before the vehicle will start, you provide a breath sample. If alcohol above a preset limit is detected, the vehicle won't start. IIDs are often required by a court or the DMV after certain DUI cases, and many drivers in Florida are told which provider to use before they ever pick up the phone. Whatever your situation, the physical install and ongoing calibrations still have to happen somewhere local — and that's where we come in.

What about ongoing calibrations?

Ignition interlock devices need periodic calibration on a set schedule to stay compliant. We handle those calibrations locally in Cape Coral, so you're not driving across the county every time one comes due. Keep your appointments on schedule and we'll keep the device reading correctly.
