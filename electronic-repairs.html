What we repair

12-volt electronics. If the engine light is on and the radio is fine, that is a mechanic. If the radio is dead, the amp is in protect, the camera is blank, or the fob quit, that is us.

  • Factory and aftermarket radios — no power, no sound, stuck on a logo, lost Bluetooth, steering-wheel controls that died after a battery pull.
  • Amplifiers — protect mode, one dead channel, overheating, factory amps that failed after a stereo install.
  • Speakers — blown voices, rattles, one door that went quiet.
  • Dash cams and backup cameras — failed cameras, cut harnesses, parking-mode that killed the battery.
  • Key fobs — battery, repair, or replacement and programming. From $15 / $35 / $100.
