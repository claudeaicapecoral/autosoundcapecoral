What can I expect from a backup camera install?

We can add a rear-view camera to almost any vehicle — a daily driver, a work truck, a classic, or a car that never came with a screen. Every install is done in-house with the wiring run clean and hidden, so the camera looks factory and the cabin stays tidy. Most vehicles are done the same day in about 1 to 3 hours.

A backup camera takes the guesswork out of reversing — you see kids, poles, curbs, and trailer hitches that mirrors miss. It is one of the most useful upgrades you can add, especially on larger trucks and SUVs with big blind spots.

Where does the camera picture show up?

You do not need a fancy dash to get a camera. We can send the picture to whatever screen fits your vehicle and budget:

  • Your factory screen: in many cases we integrate the camera into your existing factory display, so it looks and works like the manufacturer built it in.
  • A rear-view mirror monitor: a clean option for cars with no screen — the camera view appears right in a replacement mirror when you shift into reverse.
  • A new head unit: pairing the camera with a new touchscreen radio gives you a big, bright display plus Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, and better audio.

What camera styles do you offer?

We match the camera to your vehicle and the look you want:

  • License-plate mount: sits above or below your plate for an easy, clean, low-cost install.
  • Flush / bumper mount: a small camera tucked into the bumper or body for a nearly hidden, factory-clean look.

Not sure which style fits your car? Tell us your year, make, and model and how you want to view the camera, and we will point you to the right setup — no upsell pressure.

Great for trucks, RVs, and towing?

Absolutely. Big vehicles have the biggest blind spots, so a rear camera pays off most on pickups, box trucks, RVs, and vans. We also set up cameras that make hitching a trailer a one-person job and can add wireless cameras for trailers and RVs where running a wire the full length is impractical.

Can you install a camera I bought, or do you supply one?

Either way works. We are glad to install a camera you already purchased, or we can supply the right camera for your vehicle and the screen you want to use. Bring your own parts or let us handle everything — the quote is free and up front.
