What makes marine audio different from car audio?

Marine gear is engineered to survive an environment that destroys regular car electronics. The differences are in the materials:

  • Marine-rated, waterproof speakers — treated cones, rubber surrounds, and sealed baskets that shrug off spray and rinse-downs.
  • Corrosion-resistant amps and subs — conformal-coated boards and stainless hardware built to run in humidity and salt air.
  • UV- and salt-resistant head units — sealed faces and UV-stabilized housings that won't fade, crack, or short out in the sun.

We install the brands built specifically for the water — Wet Sounds, JL Audio Marine, and Fusion — so the system lasts as long as the boat.

Does my boat need a second battery?

For bigger amp and subwoofer systems, yes. A dedicated audio battery keeps your music from draining the battery you need to start the engine — so you can play at anchor all afternoon and still crank up when it's time to head in.

We set up dual-battery systems and isolators so your stereo draws from its own bank. It's the difference between a great day on the water and a call for a jump.
