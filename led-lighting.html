What we install

LED is the easiest upgrade to do badly. Cheap kits get spliced into whatever wire is closest, run without a fuse, and fail in a season — or worse, backfeed and throw dash errors. We wire lighting on its own fused circuit with proper relays and switches so it behaves like it came from the factory.

  • Truck & off-road bars — light bars and pods with proper relay wiring and a dash switch.

Is LED lighting legal in Florida?

Mostly, with limits. Florida restricts red and blue lighting visible from the front of a vehicle — those colors are reserved for emergency vehicles — and prohibits lighting that flashes or oscillates on a road-going vehicle. Interior accent lighting, white and amber exterior lighting, and underbody lighting used off-road are generally fine.

We will tell you honestly which setups keep you street-legal and which ones will get you pulled over. If you want something for show or off-road use only, we will wire it on a separate switch so you can shut it off on the road.
