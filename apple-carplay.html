What to expect

Apple CarPlay puts a familiar, driver-friendly version of your iPhone right on your dash — maps, messages, calls, music, and podcasts, all controlled by voice or a few big taps. We add CarPlay (and Android Auto) to cars, trucks, and SUVs with clean, factory-quality installs. Every job is planned around your exact vehicle so wiring is tidy, the screen fits right, and nothing rattles.

Most installs are done the same day. Before we touch your vehicle, we confirm which path it supports and quote it up front so there are no surprises.

Can I add Apple CarPlay to my older car?

Yes — in most cases. Even if your car shipped years before CarPlay existed, we can add it by installing an aftermarket head unit that supports Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. That single upgrade modernizes an older dash with a bright touchscreen, better sound options, and smartphone integration. If your vehicle has a factory screen you want to keep, ask us about factory-integration options that add CarPlay to the display you already have.

Wired vs. wireless CarPlay

We install both, and we'll help you pick the one that fits how you drive:

  • Wired CarPlay connects your iPhone through a USB cable each time you get in. It's simple, reliable, and keeps your phone charged while you drive.
  • Wireless CarPlay connects automatically over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth — your phone can stay in your pocket or bag and CarPlay just appears when you start the car.

Many systems that were originally wired can be upgraded to wireless. Tell us which you'd prefer and we'll spec it for your vehicle.

Will I keep my steering-wheel controls and backup camera?

In most vehicles, yes. With the right integration kit we retain your steering-wheel volume, track, and phone buttons so they keep working with the new system. With the correct adapter we also keep your factory backup camera so it still shows on screen when you shift into reverse. We confirm compatibility for your specific year, make, and model before we begin — and if your vehicle needs a different solution, we'll explain it before any work starts.

We also do Android Auto

Prefer Android? Every CarPlay path we install supports Android Auto too, wired or wireless. Whether your household mixes iPhones and Android phones or you just want both covered, one system handles it.
