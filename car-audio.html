What we install

As a Rockford Fosgate authorized dealer — plus JL Audio, Kenwood, and Alpine — we install and dial in every part of a car audio system:

  • Head units: touchscreen and digital-media receivers, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, backup-camera ready.
  • Component & coaxial speakers: factory-speaker upgrades that clean up your highs and mids in every door.
  • Subwoofers: single subs, sub-and-amp packages, and custom-built enclosures for real low end.
  • Amplifiers: mono, multi-channel, and full-system amps matched and wired for clean power.
  • Full custom builds: multi-amp systems, custom enclosures, and complete sound stages designed around how you drive.

How long does a car stereo install take?

It depends on the job. A basic head unit swap is usually done in about 1 to 2 hours. A full system build with subs, amps, speakers, and custom wiring runs 4 to 8 hours. Most installs are completed the same day — we'll give you a realistic time when you book.

Can you install a stereo I bought online?

Yes. We install customer-supplied gear — the head unit, speakers, subwoofer, or amp you bought online or elsewhere. Bring it in and we'll add the correct dash kit, wiring harness, and steering wheel control adapter for your vehicle so it works right the first time.

Not sure the gear you bought fits your car? Bring it by or call 239-542-8830 and we'll confirm what adapters you need before we start.
