Is my tint legal in Florida?

Florida sets how dark you can legally go, measured by how much light the film lets through (VLT):

  • Sedans: front side windows over 28% VLT; back side and rear windows over 15%.
  • SUVs & vans: front side windows over 28%; back side and rear any darkness.
  • Windshield: non-reflective tint allowed above the manufacturer's AS-1 line.

We install every job street-legal by default. If you carry a Florida medical exemption, we can go darker — bring your paperwork. Full breakdown on our Florida window tint law guide.
