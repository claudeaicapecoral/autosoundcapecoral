Why remote start matters more in Florida than up north

Remote start has a reputation as a cold-weather product. In Southwest Florida it is the opposite problem and arguably a worse one: a car parked in the sun hits interior temperatures well over 130°F, the steering wheel is untouchable, and the A/C spends your first ten minutes of driving just catching up.

Starting the vehicle three to five minutes before you walk out means the cabin is cooled and the wheel is usable by the time you open the door. It also puts less strain on your interior — heat is what cracks dashboards and fades upholstery.

What we install

The install is the whole product

Remote start touches ignition, security, and immobilizer systems. Done wrong it drains batteries, throws faults, defeats your anti-theft, or damages a module that costs more than the system did. We use vehicle-specific interface modules and T-harnesses where they exist, so the system talks to your car the way the manufacturer intended instead of being spliced into it.
