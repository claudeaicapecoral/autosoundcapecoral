Factory-look upgrades for Estero vehicles

Many Estero vehicles are newer, so the job is usually integration rather than tearing everything out. We add CarPlay to vehicles that did not ship with it, improve factory speakers without replacing every control, hardwire discreet dash cams, and install heat-rejecting ceramic tint.

We use vehicle-specific kits and interfaces, keep compatible steering-wheel controls and warning chimes working, hide the wiring, and test every feature before pickup.

Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation

We install wired or wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto so navigation, music, calls, and messages are easier to use while driving. Depending on the vehicle, the upgrade can use a replacement head unit or a vehicle-specific integration solution. Call with the year, make, model, and current screen for an exact answer.

Car stereo installation and speaker upgrades

We install head units, speakers, amplifiers, subwoofers, and backup cameras. A quality speaker upgrade—sometimes paired with a compact amplifier—can transform the sound while keeping the factory screen. We also troubleshoot no sound, intermittent power, battery drain, distorted speakers, and amplifiers stuck in protect.

Ceramic window tint for Estero heat

Window tint starts at $149 and carries a lifetime warranty against bubbling, peeling, and purple fade. Ceramic film gives the strongest heat rejection while staying signal-friendly for phones and GPS. Most full-vehicle jobs are completed the same day.

Marine audio for Estero boat owners

We install marine-rated head units, speakers, amplifiers, and subwoofers with wiring and protection built for Southwest Florida heat, humidity, salt, and spray. Tell us the boat type and current equipment for an upfront quote.
