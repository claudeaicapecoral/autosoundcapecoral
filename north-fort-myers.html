Why North Fort Myers drivers come to Auto Sound

North Fort Myers sits just across the water from us, and it is one of the easiest runs to our shop from anywhere in Lee County. We see a steady stream of trucks, work vans, daily drivers, and boats from Bayshore, Pondella, North Tamiami Trail, and the Hancock Bridge corridor.

A lot of North Fort Myers work is practical rather than showy — a factory radio that finally died, a backup camera on a towing vehicle, tint on a work truck that bakes all day, or a dash cam for a commute. We handle all of it with clean, factory-look wiring that will not rattle loose in a year.

Car stereo installation and radio troubleshooting

We install aftermarket radios, speakers, amplifiers, subwoofers, wired or wireless Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, and backup cameras. If the radio has no sound, cuts out, drains the battery, or sends an amp into protect, we check the power, wiring, speakers, amplifier, and vehicle interface before recommending parts.

Every install is matched to the vehicle. We use the correct dash kit and wiring interface, retain steering-wheel controls when compatible, hide wiring, set amplifier gain correctly, and test the system before you leave. Window tint for work trucks, vans, and daily drivers

Ceramic and carbon tint cuts glare and heat for vehicles that sit outside all day. Tint starts at $149, carries a lifetime warranty against bubbling, peeling, and purple fade, and is installed to Florida-legal limits. Most full-vehicle jobs are finished the same day, usually in two to three hours.

Backup cameras and dash cams for trucks

We add cleanly wired backup cameras to trucks, vans, and towing vehicles, including vehicles that never had a factory camera. We also hardwire front or front-and-rear dash cams so the power cable stays hidden and the camera starts with the vehicle.

Marine audio for North Fort Myers boat owners

We install marine-rated head units, speakers, amplifiers, and subwoofers built for Southwest Florida heat, humidity, salt, and spray. Tell us the boat type, current equipment, and what you want the system to do; we will quote the right amount of equipment instead of guessing.
